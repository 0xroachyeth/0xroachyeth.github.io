Changing the house for my 85-year-old grandmother

My 85-year-old grandmother can't get outside much, so I brought the sun cycle into the house: bright days, dim evenings, better air.

Full write-up coming

I'm still writing up exactly what I changed, room by room: the light in the day, the light at night, the air and the water. Until then, the idea is simple: bright during the day, dim and warm after sunset, the way the sun does it.

Be careful

Every older adult is different. Make changes with their doctor and caregivers, and watch for falls when dimming lights at night.
