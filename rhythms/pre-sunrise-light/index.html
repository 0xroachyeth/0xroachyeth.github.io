Pre-sunrise light

Your eyes set your body clock from morning light. I stand outside for the whole dawn, from the first blue light until the sun is up, and I do it every day.

Photo or Short goes here

How I do it

−60 MIN
Breakfast first

I eat about an hour before sunrise, so I'm not hungry while I'm outside.

−40 MIN
Outside for dawn

No sunglasses, no windshield, phone in my pocket. I just watch the sky change.

0 MIN
Sunrise, then move

Rope flow or a walk while the sun clears the horizon. Then I log it.

Why it works

There's solid human research that bright light in the morning shifts the body clock earlier and helps people fall asleep at night. Starting before sunrise is my own addition. I like the slow change in color, and that part is my n=1.

Be careful

Never stare straight at the sun. If you have an eye condition, are on photosensitizing medication, or have bipolar disorder, talk to your doctor first. This is what I do, not medical advice.
