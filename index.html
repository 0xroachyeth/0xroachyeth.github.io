Hi, I'm Ethan.

Stay radical. Stay feral. Never settle.

My dad got me into biohacking when I was 18, and I've been experimenting on myself ever since. Then came three knee surgeries, a foot surgery and the years I call the gap. I felt lost. I never had the clear path everyone around me seemed to have, and I went deep into some dark places with my mental health, looking for what was true. Some people would call that lazy. I see it as my body refusing to settle for an average life, and it taught me more than anything else.

I didn't find my way back in a gym. I found it outside: at sunrise, on the jiu-jitsu mats, and with a rope in my hands. And I found it inside, through inner work, mystical and esoteric wisdom, and a mentor, a mystic with deep knowledge, life experience and wisdom, who helped me regain my power and keep my essence. I still question everything, and I'm still seeking. That's my story.

Most mornings I'm outside about 40 minutes before the sun comes up, and I write every one down in my Sunrise Log. The things I keep coming back to, like light, real food, breath and movement, are my rhythms. They're not rules, just what I fall into. When I want to know if something actually works, I run it as an experiment and publish what happened, flops included.

Fewer supplements, less pressure, more sun. If you want company finding your own rhythm, you can walk with me one-on-one. Or start free with the 7-Day Sunrise Reset.

This is my own experience, not medical advice.
