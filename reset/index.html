The Sunrise Reset

One small change each morning for a week. No supplements, no tracking, no pressure. Each day keeps the one before it, so by Day 7 you'll have a morning that's yours.

Here's the whole week. Do one a day, in order. If you miss a day, just pick it back up.

1234567
Day 1

Get outside at first light

Tomorrow, step outside right before or as the sun comes up. Ten minutes is enough. No sunglasses, no window in the way, phone in your pocket. Just watch the sky change.

WHY Morning light is the strongest signal your body clock gets. It tells it what time it is.

KEEP IT EASY Cloudy counts. Late counts. You don't have to be up at 5. Just get out when you wake.

Day 2

Eat before you scroll

Keep Day 1, and add this: eat real food early. Eggs, meat, fish, fruit, whatever you have. Eat it before coffee and before the phone.

WHY An early, real breakfast tells your body the day has started. Coffee on an empty stomach doesn't.

KEEP IT EASY No macros, no rules. Just food that grew or had a mother.

Day 3

Leave the phone down for the first hour

Keep it on the charger, in another room if you need to. The first hour is yours: light, food, quiet.

WHY Whatever you let in first sets the tone. The feed can wait.

KEEP IT EASY If an hour is too much, start with 20 minutes. Notice what you reach for.

Day 4

Put your bare feet on the ground

While you're outside, take your shoes off and stand on grass, dirt or sand for a few minutes.

WHY This one is mostly my n=1, but it brings me into my body faster than anything. Try it and see.

KEEP IT EASY When it's cold, one minute counts. Skip it if you have numb feet, diabetes or poor circulation.

Day 5

Move a little in the morning light

Ten minutes of easy movement outside: a walk, rope flow, shaking out your arms. Nothing that needs a warm-up.

WHY Moving outside early stacks the light with blood flow. After three knee surgeries, this is how I start every day.

KEEP IT EASY Gentle on purpose. It's a wake-up, not a workout.

Day 6

Breathe slow, outside

Stand or sit still for five minutes. Breathe in and out through your nose, slower than feels normal, and watch the light.

WHY Slow breathing is one of the quickest ways to calm your nervous system down.

KEEP IT EASY If you get lightheaded, breathe normally. There's nothing to win here.

Day 7

Watch the sun go down

Catch the sunset tonight, then keep the lights low and screens dim until bed. Then look back at the week.

WHY Evening light closes the loop the morning opened. Dimmer nights tend to make for easier sleep.

KEEP IT EASY Which day felt best? Keep that one. That's your rhythm. You don't need all seven.

After Day 7

Keep going

See what I do every morning in the Sunrise Log, or read my rhythms. If you want company finding your own, you can walk with me one-on-one. And if you want to be around people living this way, join the waitlist for The Grove.

Be careful

Never stare straight at the sun. If you have an eye condition, take photosensitizing medication, or have a heart, lung or blood sugar condition, talk to your doctor first. This is what I do, not medical advice.
